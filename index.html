Jarv Mail

Jarv Mail is a private, single-user integration that lets its owner's personal assistant read the owner's own Google account data (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets and Contacts) through Google's official APIs.

It is not a public service. It has one user, who is also its developer and operator, and it is not offered to anyone else.

What it does

  • Searches and reads mail, calendar events and documents in the owner's account when the owner asks the assistant to.
  • Runs entirely on hardware the owner controls.
