Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Jarv Mail is a private, single-user integration. Its only user is its owner, who is also its developer and operator. This policy describes how it handles Google account data.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent, Jarv Mail accesses the owner's own Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets and Google Contacts data through Google's APIs.

How the data is used

The data is used only to answer the owner's own requests to their personal assistant, such as finding a message or checking a calendar. It is not used for advertising, and it is not used to train generalized machine-learning models.

Storage

Authorization tokens are stored in an encrypted keyring on hardware the owner controls. Google account data is fetched on demand and is not copied to any third-party storage service.

Sharing

Google account data is not sold, and is not shared with or transferred to any third party, except as needed to provide the functionality described above to the owner or as required by law.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Jarv Mail's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account third-party access page. Revoking access invalidates the stored tokens.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on the project issue tracker.
